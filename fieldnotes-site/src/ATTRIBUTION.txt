Adaptations: subset to models annotated Colorectal Adenocarcinoma; join model metadata,
five gene-effect columns (WRN, BRAF, KRAS, PCNA, RPL4), and continuous MSIScore by
ModelID; calculate descriptive WRN Spearman correlation. Retain missing observations.
Full source download URLs, provider MD5 checksums and byte sizes are included in
continuous-evidence.json and in exported dossiers. No endorsement is implied.

Historical manuscript MSI labels, manuscript-derived group contrasts, and TCGA data
are not included in this Site. Cell-model association is not clinical efficacy.

Additional adaptation: WRN sensitivity using complete measurement pairs aggregated
by source donor, with separate medians for MSI score and gene effect.
