Data attribution and adaptations

This view describes association within laboratory cell models. It does not infer MSI categories, treatment response or clinical benefit. The same models underpin the historical reanalysis; this is not an independent replication.

Sources & analytical choices

WRN is the prespecified gene. BRAF, KRAS, PCNA and RPL4 are descriptive comparators. Spearman correlation is reported only for WRN. Missing values remain missing. Choose individual models or donor medians in the chart. Donor aggregation uses only complete measurement pairs from the same model. A separate WRN sensitivity panel compares both units. Gene-effect axes are shared across the five genes. All records derive from the CC BY 4.0 DepMap release; the manuscript annotations are not included.
